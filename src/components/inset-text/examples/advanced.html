NHS Health Check Service

The NHS Health Check is a free checkup for adults in England aged 40 to 74.

Important eligibility information:

You're eligible for a free NHS Health Check if you're aged between 40 and 74 and don't have a pre-existing condition like heart disease or diabetes.

The health check includes checks for diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease, stroke and dementia.

Code Snippet Example

When implementing custom accessibility features, use the following approach:

const insetText = createInsetText({
  text: 'Your content here',
  hiddenLabel: 'Custom label: ',
  classes: 'custom-styling'
})

This ensures proper accessibility and customization options.
