Notification Banner Component - Basic Examples

Default Notification Banner

A basic notification banner with standard styling for general information.

System Notification

This is a standard notification banner with default styling. It provides important information to users without being overly prominent.

This is regular page content that appears below the notification.

Success Notification

A success notification for positive feedback and confirmations.

Success

Your settings have been saved successfully. All changes are now active.

Success notifications use green styling and a "status" ARIA role for screen readers.

Warning Notification

A warning notification to alert users about potential issues or required actions.

Action Required

Your session will expire in 10 minutes due to inactivity. Please save your work to avoid losing changes.

Warning notifications use amber styling and an "alert" ARIA role for immediate attention.

Error Notification

An error notification for communicating problems that need user attention.

Error

Unable to save your changes due to a connection error. Please check your internet connection and try again.

Error notifications use red styling with high contrast for critical issues.

Info Notification

An info notification for helpful tips, updates, or additional information.

Information

System maintenance is scheduled for tonight from 2:00 AM to 4:00 AM. Some features may be temporarily unavailable.

Info notifications use blue styling for informational content that isn't urgent.

Dismissible Notification

A notification with a close button that users can dismiss.

Did you know?

You can customize your dashboard by dragging and dropping widgets. Try it out!

Dismissible notifications include a close button for user control. Click the × to dismiss.

HTML Content Notification

A notification that contains HTML content including links and formatting.

Account Verification

Please verify your email address to enable all features.

Didn't receive the email? Resend verification email.

HTML content allows for rich formatting, links, and structured information within notifications.

Notification with List Content

A notification containing a list of items or multiple pieces of information.

Validation Errors

Please correct the following errors:

  • Email address is required
  • Password must be at least 8 characters
  • Terms and conditions must be accepted

List content is commonly used for form validation errors or multiple related messages.

Compact Notification

A compact notification with reduced padding for less prominent messaging.

Saved

Draft automatically saved at 3:45 PM

Compact notifications take up less space and are suitable for frequent or less critical updates.

Notification Without Title

A simple notification with just content and no separate title.

New feature available: You can now export your data in multiple formats from the settings page.

Notifications don't always need a separate title - content can be self-contained.
